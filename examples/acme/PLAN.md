## Engine plans

### 03 Manual Outbound + Cold Calling · $7,970 · Owner: Dana (AE lead)
Why it is on: ACV clears the $25K bar for tiered, rep-led outbound
(MO-5, an operator heuristic). Already running per intake. Funded at
$7,000 this cycle by CEO override; see Approved overrides.
First 90 days: re-tier the list against the Dream-150 ladder (MO-4, an
operator heuristic); buy verified mobiles before buying training
(MO-1); hold three attempts per prospect as the floor (MO-2).
The bar: 0.27 percent dial-to-meeting, roughly one booked meeting per
370 dials (MO-3).
Tripwire: connect rate stuck under the 10 percent per-dial measure
while effort holds means buy better numbers, not more dials (MO-1).

### 01 Automated Outbound · $5,312 · Owner: Sam (GTM engineer)
Why it is on: baseline layer; one owner exists.
First 90 days: secondary domains and warmup weeks 1 to 4 (AO-3);
first campaigns at low volume weeks 5 to 8; scale toward full operator
throughput weeks 9 to 12 (AO-4, an operator heuristic).
The bar: 17 to 18 percent replies with advanced personalization, against
a 3.43 percent platform average (AO-1).
Tripwire: replies sliding toward the platform average means stop and
fix the list or the angle before touching volume (AO-1); bounce above
2 percent means no sends until the waterfall is fixed (AO-2).

### 04 ABM · $5,312 · Owner: Dana (AE lead)
Why it is on: enterprise ACV and reps to route to.
First 90 days: named list and tiers weeks 1 to 4; entry signals per
awareness stage weeks 5 to 8 (ABM-3); route by stage weeks 9 to 12.
Size the outreach off person-level visitor match at 8 to 15 percent,
not the 30 to 65 percent company-level number (ABM-1).
The bar: 25 to 40 percent of the named list engaging inside 90 days
(ABM-2, an operator heuristic; no external source supports it).
Tripwire: any account advancing a stage on a single signal means the
honesty rule broke; tighten entry criteria the same day.

### 08 Social Content · $2,655 · Owner: Alex (CEO)
Why it is on: the founder will post.
First 90 days: one lane, two weeks of posts banked, then three a week
plus daily comments; log repeat engagers into outbound.
The bar: personal profiles engaging at about 1.5x the company page
(SC-1); inbound conversations roughly tripling inside 60 days is an
operator heuristic, not a benchmark (SC-2).
Tripwire: cadence below three a week breaks the compounding; fix the
draft pipeline, not the ambition.

### 05 Community + Partner Led · $1,875 · Owner: Alex (CEO)
What gets built this quarter: marketplace listing plus two or three
co-sell relationships; quiet capture of who shows up and asks.
Graduation gate: channel clearing 25 percent of new business at the
year mark (CP-3, an operator heuristic).

### 07 SEO + AEO · $1,875 · Owner: Sam (GTM engineer)
What gets built this quarter: topic clusters and the first versus
pages (SEO-1); the "how did you hear about us" field; the weekly
prompt set (SEO-3).
Graduation gate: AI-referred traffic converting at least at parity with
organic, which is the 1.05x B2B SaaS figure in SEO-2, measured on our
own funnel.
