## Engine plans

### 03 Manual Outbound + Cold Calling · $5,795 · Owner: Dana (AE lead)
Why it is on: ACV clears the $25K bar for tiered, rep-led outbound
(MO-5, an operator heuristic). Already running per intake.
First 90 days: re-tier the list against the Dream-150 ladder; buy
verified mobiles before buying training (MO-1); hold the eight-attempt
discipline (MO-2).
The bar: 2 to 3 percent dial-to-meeting; coached teams on clean data
run 5 to 8 (MO-3).
Tripwire: connect rate stuck at the 5 percent average while effort
holds means buy better numbers, not more dials (MO-1).

### 01 Automated Outbound · $3,865 · Owner: Sam (GTM engineer)
Why it is on: baseline layer; one owner exists.
First 90 days: secondary domains and warmup weeks 1 to 4 (AO-3);
first campaigns at low volume weeks 5 to 8; scale toward full operator
throughput weeks 9 to 12 (AO-4).
The bar: 6 to 12 percent replies enriched (AO-1).
Tripwire: replies in the 1 to 3 band means stop and fix the list or
the angle before touching volume (AO-1); verification under 95 percent
means no sends until the waterfall is fixed (AO-2).

### 04 ABM · $3,862 · Owner: Dana (AE lead)
Why it is on: enterprise ACV and reps to route to.
First 90 days: named list and tiers weeks 1 to 4; entry signals per
awareness stage weeks 5 to 8 (ABM-3); route by stage weeks 9 to 12.
The bar: 25 to 40 percent of the named list engaging inside 90 days
(ABM-2).
Tripwire: any account advancing a stage on a single signal means the
honesty rule broke; tighten entry criteria the same day.

### 09 Events · $3,862 · Owner: Alex (CEO)
Why it is on: enterprise ICP and budget above the $15K floor (an
operator-heuristic knob).
First 90 days: pick one or two ICP-dense events; build the attendee
list six weeks out (EV-1); pre-book half the meetings (EV-4); hold the
24/48-hour SLA (EV-6).
The bar: ICP-first programs run $2.5K to $5K per opportunity (EV-3).
Tripwire: fewer than half the meetings pre-booked at doors-open means
next quarter goes ICP-first or nowhere.

### 08 Social Content · $1,932 · Owner: Alex (CEO)
Why it is on: the founder will post.
First 90 days: one lane, two weeks of posts banked, then three a week
plus daily comments; log repeat engagers into outbound.
The bar: inbound conversations roughly tripling inside 60 days (SC-2).
Tripwire: cadence below three a week breaks the compounding; fix the
draft pipeline, not the ambition.

### 06 Paid Media · $1,932 · Owner: Sam (GTM engineer)
Why it is on: the ABM named list is live and budget clears the $8K
floor (PM-5, an operator heuristic).
First 90 days: matched audiences from the named list (PM-4);
thought-leader creative (PM-1); demo asks only at warm retargeting.
The bar: cycles 15 to 30 percent faster against the named list (PM-2).
Tripwire: a demo ask running at a cold audience is the worst cell in
the matrix; kill the ad set the day you find it.

### 05 Community + Partner Led · $1,875 · Owner: Alex (CEO)
What gets built this quarter: marketplace listing plus two or three
co-sell relationships; quiet capture of who shows up and asks.
Graduation gate: channel clearing 25 percent of new business at the
year mark (CP-3, an operator heuristic).

### 07 SEO + AEO · $1,875 · Owner: Sam (GTM engineer)
What gets built this quarter: topic clusters and the first versus
pages (SEO-1); the "how did you hear about us" field; the weekly
prompt set (SEO-3).
Graduation gate: AI-referred traffic converting at the SEO-2 bar,
measured on our own funnel.
