# Engine 07 · SEO + AEO

The compounding retrieval surface: topic clusters for buyers, versus
pages for shortlists, structure that language models can extract. Months
to instrument, then it pays on every query forever.

## The flow

↳ Keyword + topic research
↳ AI content drafting
↳ Structuring it for LLM answers as well as SERPs
↳ Ranking + traffic capture
↳ Lead capture + CRM sync

## How the best run it

Vanta is the reference case: topic clusters organized around what buyers
research, comparison and versus pages for the shortlist prompts, and
dense internal linking so a model can trace the whole argument. The data
says versus pages are the content type most correlated with AI-search
traffic, and B2B sites with 21 or more comparison pages show 900 percent
more median AI search sessions than sites with one to five. Content
explains only about 28 percent of the variance, so brand authority and
category fit carry the rest. Structure for extraction: question-shaped
headings, FAQ schema, answers in the first sentences. Then measure what
analytics hides: AI-referred clicks often land as Direct because AI
platforms do not consistently pass a referrer, and no source quantifies
how often, so the "how did you hear about us" field on the demo form is
the attribution layer and a fixed prompt set run weekly is the rank
tracker.

## The number

For a B2B SaaS audience, AI-referred visitors convert at 1.05x organic,
which is parity, and AI referrals run 0.2 to 3 percent of sessions.
Instrument the channel because it is growing and because versus pages pay
on both surfaces, not because the traffic converts better. Anyone quoting
a multiplier above 2x for B2B SaaS is quoting a retail or IT-services
cohort.

## Stack

Ahrefs (keyword and cluster research) · Claude (drafting) · AirOps
(content operations) · Warmly (visitor capture)

## Run it when

Always instrument now; the engine has no fast mode and waiting only
moves the payoff further out. Budget allocation stays small; the real
costs are editorial discipline and patience.

## First 90 days

Weeks 1 to 4: map the clusters around what buyers actually research.
List every versus and comparison page the shortlist implies.
Weeks 5 to 8: ship the first cluster with question-shaped headings, FAQ
schema, and answers in the first sentences. Dense internal links.
Weeks 9 to 12: add the "how did you hear about us" field, stand up the
fixed weekly prompt set, and keep shipping comparison pages toward the
21-page tier.

## Tripwires

Anyone forecasting pipeline from this engine inside the first months:
the clock is wrong, not the engine. Attribution reading zero while
Direct traffic climbs: the measurement layer is missing, not the
results; fix the form field before judging the content.
