# Engine 05 · Community + Partner Led

The slow flywheel. Practitioners get real value with no purchase in
sight, or partners get tiers, certifications, and co-sell economics.
Either lane produces nothing this quarter and compounds for years.

## The flow

↳ Strategy (community-led or partner-led)
↳ Community and partner build
↳ Value engine: exclusive access, certifications, networking, co-branded content
↳ Signal capture + routing
↳ Flywheel reinforcement

## How the best run it

Pick one lane first; the flywheels are different machines. Community-led
means practitioners get real value with no purchase anywhere in sight,
and the pipeline signal is who shows up, asks, and answers. Partner-led
means tiers, certifications, co-marketing funds, and marketplace
listings; Wiz grew its integration network past 300 partners by running
it inside product rather than sales, and dbt's marketplace transactions
grew 190 percent in a year once cloud-marketplace buying unlocked stuck
procurement. Either lane, the value engine has to pay members before it
pays you: certifications people put on their profile, access they cannot
get elsewhere. Capture is quiet: who attended, who asked, who brought a
colleague, routed to a human only when the signal stacks.

## The number

This quarter it produces nothing; run it for the year and channel
clearing 25 percent of new business is the graduation bar.

Sources: claims CP-1 to CP-3 in docs/SOURCES.md (evidence class and
confidence per claim; unsourced values are Andrew operator heuristics).

## Stack

Slack (community home) · Circle (structured community) · Luma (events
and gatherings)

## Run it when

Instrument now in almost every case; the cost is mostly patience. A
practitioner-facing or self-serve product leans community-led; otherwise
run the partner lane (marketplace listing plus two or three co-sell
relationships). Defer only when nobody can host it; a dead community is
worse than none.

## First 90 days

Weeks 1 to 4: pick the lane and name the owner. Community: pick the home
and seed it with people who already like you. Partner: list the
marketplace and shortlist co-sell candidates.
Weeks 5 to 8: ship the first value: a certification, an exclusive
session, a co-branded asset that pays members before it pays you.
Weeks 9 to 12: wire quiet capture (attendance, questions, referrals) and
route to a human only when the signal stacks.

## Tripwires

Anyone judging this engine on a quarterly clock: reset expectations or
shut it down; those are the only honest options. Channel under the 25
percent graduation bar at the year mark: keep or fold is now a deliberate
decision, not a drift.
