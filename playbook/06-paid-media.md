# Engine 06 · Paid Media

An accelerant, never the creator. Paid rarely makes category demand; run
against a named list with full-funnel creative it shortens cycles and
carries a real share of pipeline. The list is the strategy.

## The flow

↳ ICP + target account list
↳ Ads strategy: funnel plan, creative, media buying
↳ Nurture sequences + remarketing
↳ Engagement tracking
↳ Conversion + CRM sync

## How the best run it

The list is the strategy. Matched audiences of 300 to 1,500 named
accounts; the 50-account list sales loves is algorithm sabotage. Creative
runs full funnel: thought-leader ads from a real person at the top
(roughly double the click-through of brand ads at half the cost per
qualified lead), proof and comparisons to engagers in the middle, and the
demo ask reserved for warm retargeting pools only. Demo offers at cold
audiences are the worst cell in the whole matrix. The other half of the
engine is speed to lead: a form submission that books a meeting on the
spot converts several times better than one that waits a day in the CRM.

## The number

Paid rarely creates category demand; run well against a named list it
accelerates cycles 15 to 30 percent and carries 5 to 15 percent of
pipeline.

## Stack

LinkedIn Ads (delivery) · Chili Piper (speed to lead) · Ergo (signal
and conversion sync)

## Run it when

The ABM named list exists and monthly budget clears about $8K; under
that, LinkedIn spend fragments below the learning threshold and the
honest move is to bank it. Paid without a named list is spray.

## First 90 days

Weeks 1 to 4: build matched audiences from the named list (300 minimum).
Draft thought-leader creative from a real person, not the brand page.
Weeks 5 to 8: full-funnel structure live: awareness to engagers to warm
retargeting. Demo asks nowhere except the warm pool.
Weeks 9 to 12: wire instant booking on the form. Judge on qualified
meetings and cycle speed against the named list, not on clicks.

## Tripwires

A demo ask running at a cold audience: the worst cell in the matrix;
kill the ad set the day you find it. Audience under 300 accounts: the
algorithm cannot learn; widen the list or pause the spend.
