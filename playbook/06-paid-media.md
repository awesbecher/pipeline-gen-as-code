# Engine 06 · Paid Media

An accelerant, never the creator. Paid rarely makes category demand; run
against a named list with full-funnel creative it shortens cycles and
carries a real share of pipeline. The list is the strategy.

## The flow

↳ ICP + target account list
↳ Ads strategy: funnel plan, creative, media buying
↳ Nurture sequences + remarketing
↳ Engagement tracking
↳ Conversion + CRM sync

## How the best run it

The list is the strategy. LinkedIn's own documentation sets the hard
floor at 300 members per campaign audience; the 50-account list sales
loves cannot even run. The 1,500-account upper bound is Andrew's
heuristic, not a LinkedIn number. Creative runs full funnel:
thought-leader ads from a real person at the top, proof and comparisons
to engagers in the middle, and the demo ask reserved for warm
retargeting pools only. Thought Leader Ads are reported at 4.65 percent
CTR and $0.51 CPC against 0.68 percent and $2.42 for standard LinkedIn
ads, every one of those figures published by vendors who sell the
format, and no cost-per-qualified-lead comparison exists at all. Demo
offers at cold audiences are the worst cell in the whole matrix. The
other half of the engine is speed to lead: the MIT lead response study
measured the odds of qualifying a lead dropping 21 times between a
5-minute and a 30-minute response, and the odds of contacting one
dropping 100 times. That study is from 2007, so carry the direction with
confidence and the multipliers with a date stamp.

## The number

Paid rarely creates category demand. The two numbers usually attached to
this engine are Andrew operator heuristics with no resolvable source:
cycles accelerating 15 to 30 percent when paid runs against a named
list, and paid carrying 5 to 15 percent of pipeline. Measure the first
on your own funnel; read the second as a budget opinion.

Sources: claims PM-1 to PM-6 in docs/SOURCES.md (evidence class and
confidence per claim; unsourced values are Andrew operator heuristics).

## Stack

LinkedIn Ads (delivery) · Chili Piper (speed to lead) · Ergo (signal
and conversion sync)

## Run it when

The ABM engine is running (list built AND routed to reps) and monthly
budget clears about $8K; under that, LinkedIn spend fragments below the
learning threshold and the honest move is to bank it. Paid without a
named list is spray, and paid against a list nobody routes is spray
with better targeting.

## First 90 days

Weeks 1 to 4: build matched audiences from the named list (300 members
minimum, LinkedIn's published floor; allow up to 48 hours for the first
match).
Draft thought-leader creative from a real person, not the brand page.
Weeks 5 to 8: full-funnel structure live: awareness to engagers to warm
retargeting. Demo asks nowhere except the warm pool.
Weeks 9 to 12: wire instant booking on the form. Judge on qualified
meetings and cycle speed against the named list, not on clicks.

## Tripwires

A demo ask running at a cold audience: the worst cell in the matrix;
kill the ad set the day you find it. Audience under LinkedIn's 300-member
floor: the campaign will not run at all; widen the list or pause the
spend.
